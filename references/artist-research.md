# Artist and Adjacent-Style Research

This file records external research used to understand the board. Use it to extract shared, observable design mechanisms—not to request a direct imitation of one living artist.

## Confirmed or closely related practitioners

### Asahi Nagata

- The local board includes `88. Ridwell_yard_sign_-_Asahi_Nagata.png`.
- Her portfolio describes broad work across editorial, books, products, and campaigns. Interviews describe a digital process informed by traditional printmaking and vintage print restoration.
- Useful mechanisms: ordinary scenes remain specific; bold color masses carry hierarchy; print texture supports rather than replaces shape; people, animals, and objects can coexist without realistic depth.
- Sources: [official work portfolio](https://illoirro.com/work), [Behance portfolio](https://www.behance.net/nagataae6d8), [printmaking interview](https://awwyours.com/blogs/journal/printmaking-with-a-digital-twist-interview-with-asahi-nagata), [artist profile](https://www.blueq.com/pages/asahi-nagata).

### Paca Kim

- The local board includes `19. 찜닭_일러스트_by_김파카.jpg`.
- Her published work includes Korean food, breakfast/coffee, gardening, books, and public illustration.
- Useful mechanisms: a dish is preserved through vessel, ingredient marks, preparation relationship, and cultural context; handwritten labels support the image rather than replacing it.
- Sources: [portfolio](https://www.behance.net/kimpaca), [My Favorite Korean Food project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/84439597/My-favorite-Korean-food).

### Min Pin / Penny Min Ferguson

- The local board includes `69. Forest.jpg`, signed MINPIN.
- The portfolio spans expressive animal illustration, ceramics, textile, packaging, and product work.
- Useful mechanisms: species are simplified through distinct body masses rather than one mascot template; animal groups use variable scale, habitat fragments, and uneven spacing; craft thinking carries across media.
- Sources: [official portfolio](https://www.minpindesign.com/portfolio), [about](https://www.minpindesign.com/about), [prints archive](https://www.minpindesign.com/prints).

### Mogu Takahashi

- Closely related to the board's person/animal/object language.
- Her gallery includes people, poodles, plants, kettles, meals, horses, and cats; listed works include gouache on wood.
- Useful mechanisms: an ordinary action or relationship remains recognizable while anatomy and scale become awkward; narrative comes from pose and proximity rather than detailed faces or cinematic space.
- Material boundary: retain this as research about semantic gesture only. The studied works are gouache/brush dominant and are not included in the active generator reference set; do not attach them to image generation.
- Sources: [official gallery](https://mogutakahashi.com/gallery-page/), [about](https://mogutakahashi.com/about/), [Action — gouache on wood](https://mogutakahashi.com/portfolio-item/action/).

### Atelier Bingo

- Closely related to the board's cut shape, abstract, pattern, and print branches.
- The duo describes experimenting with screen printing, collage, and other graphic techniques to make colorful abstract work. Their personal archive includes daily-life scenes recomposed through collage.
- Useful mechanisms: build images layer by layer and color by color; use cut/assembled shapes as a thinking method; allow abstraction when the content is force, memory, or rhythm; do not apply abstraction indiscriminately to identity-dependent figures or scenes.
- Sources: [studio information](https://atelier-bingo.fr/Information-1), [personal work](https://atelier-bingo.fr/Personal-work), [studio interview](https://www.thefuturepositive.com/projects/atelier-bingo/).

### Minh Phương

- The local board includes `113. minh_phuong.png`.
- Her portfolio spans illustration, visual merchandise, branding, and editorial work.
- Useful mechanisms: combine product/food identity with energetic framing, large flat color, and decorative rhythm; preserve the central object while surrounding activity is compressed into graphic marks.
- Source: [Behance profile](https://www.behance.net/minhphuong246).

### Agathe Singer

- Adjacent to the board's portrait, plant, animal, and still-life branches.
- She describes bold color, rounded forms, and a deliberate element of strangeness; her portfolio covers portraits, animals, flowers, vases, and editorial work.
- Useful mechanisms: color and rounded mass can remain bold without becoming generic kawaii; a slightly strange silhouette or scale relation can carry adult editorial judgment.
- Sources: [official portfolio](https://agathesinger.com/), [about](https://agathesinger.com/about).

### Yeye Weller

- Adjacent to the board's humorous character and bold poster branches.
- Interviews emphasize bold colors, comic/cartoon sources, anthropomorphic animals, and short verbal jokes.
- Useful mechanisms: when humor is the source identity, keep the joke/action and use title as a shape; do not transfer anthropomorphic characters into unrelated subjects.
- Sources: [official site](https://www.yeyeweller.com/), [artist interview](https://goldapple.studio/topiamagazine/artist-yeye-weller-face-this/).

### Miroco Machiko

- Adjacent to the board's expressive animal and nature branch.
- Exhibition writing describes animals and plants through bold strokes and dazzling colors that are humorous, mysterious, child-like, and sophisticated.
- Useful mechanisms: animal energy can come from stroke mass, scale, and color rather than cute face detail; nature can be both symbolic and specific.
- Source: [exhibition highlight](https://mirocomachiko-cm.exhibit.jp/highlight/).

## Cross-artist synthesis

The repeated transferable mechanisms are:

1. Preserve the subject's identity and relationship before changing its surface.
2. Use optical mass, proportion, and diagnostic features instead of realistic anatomy.
3. Let color areas organize the image; texture stays subordinate to silhouette and hierarchy.
4. Flatten depth through stacking, grouping, enclosure, or panels—not by deleting all context.
5. Use medium evidence selectively: ink density, halftone breakup, dot fields, registration shift, or sparse pencil marks.
6. Keep action, humor, social grouping, habitat, or serving relationships when they are the actual subject.
7. Allow child-like directness, but retain adult composition, typography, and information judgment.

## Local official reference corpus

The active corpus contains 37 role-specific works under `ref_artists_official_2026-08-04/`, with per-artist contact sheets and a source manifest at `references/official-artist-reference-manifest.tsv`. Brush-dominant and mixed-media candidates are intentionally absent from the active Skill.

This corpus is deliberately routed by function. It is not one blended artist-style pack: Asahi supports print hierarchy and multi-subject scenes; Paca supports meaningful object/food relationships; Min Pin supports animals and groups; Atelier Bingo supports modular composition, saturated palette, and print layering. Mogu remains research evidence only and is not active generation input. Subject appearance must continue to come from the user's content source.

## Research boundaries

- Do not put a living artist's name into the final image-generation prompt as a style command.
- Use at least two independent visual references when learning a branch.
- Describe observable properties: shape count, scale, crop, line behavior, palette roles, material, density, and type hierarchy.
- Do not copy a signature character, exact composition, logo, title treatment, or branded campaign asset.
